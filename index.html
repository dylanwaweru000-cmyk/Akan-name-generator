Akan-Name-Generator

A simple app that generates traditional Akan day-names (like Kwame, Kofi, Ama, Esi) based on the day of the week a person was born, following the Akan naming tradition from Ghana. Users input a birth date or day, and the app returns the corresponding day-name along with its meaning.
